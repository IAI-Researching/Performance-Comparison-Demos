Benchmark: vehicle-dynamics/f1-night-duel

Intervention: Zero human-in-the-loop

Generated by `baseline/gen_parameters.py` from `telemetry.json` and the orchestrator's `build_command()`.
Commands are the current (protocol v1.1) invocations. Rows marked v1.0 ran an earlier variant of the
isolation setup; see PROTOCOL.md. Harness versions below are the versions recorded for each published row.
